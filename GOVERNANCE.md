# Governance (lightweight, phase 0–1)

The full governance charter is Section 20 of the master document. Until there is a community to govern, this repository uses the minimum that the charter's constitutional rules require.

- **Maintainer.** One maintainer merges PRs and records challenge outcomes. Additional maintainers are added by PR to this file.
- **Decisions.** Anything that changes how the repository operates is recorded as a short decision record in `docs/decisions/` (one file per decision, numbered).
- **Rules that a maintainer does not change alone** (from Section 20.2): registration of claims is free and unrestricted; no one is paid per favorable verdict; researchers own their workflow logs; record formats are open. Changing these requires a decision record and a period of public comment on the PR of at least 30 days.
- **Conflicts.** Anyone recording a challenge outcome on a claim they authored states so on the issue; another maintainer records it if one exists.
- **Appeals.** Reopen the issue with `appeal` label; a different maintainer reviews; the outcome is recorded.
- **Contribution record.** Generated quarterly from git, published in `contributions/RECORD.md`.

When the community is large enough for the charter's bodies to exist, this file is replaced by an adoption of Section 20.
